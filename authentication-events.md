## [Windows Lab]

## // Logon Monitoring

## What I practiced

 I filtered Windows Security logs for Event IDs 4624 and 4625 to check successful and failed login attempts.

## What I learned

 Event ID 4624 shows a successful logon, while 4625 shows a failed logon. In this test, I generated 2 failed attempts and 1 successful login.

## SOC Relevance

 Checking login events helps me spot repeated failures, unusual account activity and possible unauthorized access attempts.
